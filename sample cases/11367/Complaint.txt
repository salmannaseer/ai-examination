Delay in payment of service dues of complainant's father

Alleged delay in payment of service dues in respect of father of the complainant. Request for intervention.

Findings: The above proceedings indicate that the complainant is not eager to pursue his complaint because he is not responding to process issued from this office.  


Decision: In view of the above findings, I, u/s 11 of the Establishment of the Office of the Ombudsman for the Province of Sindh Act, 1991 hereby direct the Metropolitan Commissioner, Karachi Metropolitan Corporation and the Municipal Commissioner, Town Municipal Corporation Orangi to process and finalize the service dues payable to the heirs of the deceased employee and to release the dues in accordance with the policy finalized by the High Court and the Ombudsman Sindh. They are also directed to process the request for appointment against deceased quota as per prevalent rules and regulations.
